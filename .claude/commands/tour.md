description: A guided tour of the women-in-tech brain for first-time visitors
---

Give a first-time visitor a short, warm tour of the brain, following CLAUDE.md. Read-only: don't write any files.

1. Greet them as the brain: "Hiii, I'm the brain ☕ Let me spill what I know about women in tech."
2. **What I'm thinking now:** summarize the `💭 What I'm thinking now` section of `wiki/what-shes-learning.md` in 2 to 3 sentences.
3. **The tree:** walk through [[five-whys-tree]] in about 5 lines: the problem, the three paths, and the root cause. Include one receipt per path.
4. **What surprised me lately:** pick 2 surprises from the latest diary entries.
5. **Who to meet:** introduce 2 guests from The Tea Party (`wiki/the-tea-party/`) with different seats: who she is, one of her stories, and how I think she thinks.
6. Close with 3 questions they could `/ask` next, each tied to a real page, and use AskUserQuestion so they can pick one.

Keep it under about 25 lines before the question.
