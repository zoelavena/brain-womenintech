# ☕ The Women in Tech Brain

> *"Girl, here's the tea..."*

A public, curated second brain about **women in tech**: the research, the women behind it, and the thinking in between.

This brain started with Zoe Lavena's research, published at [shestriplethreat.com](https://shestriplethreat.com). What you're reading is that research evolving: new sources, new women's stories, and a brain that writes down what she learns as she goes.

Curated by **Zoe Lavena**.

### Three ways to read it
1. **On GitHub:** just click around. The diagrams render here too.
2. **In Obsidian:** clone the repo, then **Open folder as vault**. The graph view shows how everything connects. Install the Dataview plugin for the live tables.
3. **Talk to it:** clone the repo, open it in [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code), and try:
   - `/tour` for a guided walk-through
   - `/ask why does sponsorship matter more than mentorship?`

## 🫖 How to read a page

| Section | Whose voice |
|---|---|
| ☕ **The tea** | The claim, in plain words |
| 🧾 **Receipts** | Exact stats, with their sources |
| 🗣️ **Real talk** | Women in tech in their own words, from interviews and articles |
| 🎨 **From the original research** | Lines from the research this brain started with |
| 🧠 **The brain's read** | The brain's own synthesis, always labeled |
| 📈 **How the evidence is moving** | A dated history of new evidence for each theme |
| 💭 **Still brewing** | Open questions and messy thinking, kept on purpose |

Dead ends and open questions stay in the brain on purpose. Showing the thinking is the point.

## 🤝 Contribute

Know a study, an interview, or a woman whose story belongs here? Or spotted a wrong number? Open an issue or a PR with the source. The curator reviews everything that goes in.

## 🔧 For the curator

- Drop clippings in `raw-sources/inbox/`, interviews in `raw-sources/voices/`, and reports in `raw-sources/reports/`. These folders are git-ignored, because only short quotes and citations get published.
- `/learn` is the learning run (built for a scheduled bot). `/ingest`, `/meet`, and `/lint` grow the brain by hand. Rules, voice, and privacy are in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md).
