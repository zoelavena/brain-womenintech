# 📖 Case studies

Women whose stories the research uses as examples, from pioneers to today. Unlike [[the-tea-party]], this isn't a curated library. A woman lands here because her story *shows* something on the [[five-whys-tree]]. Historical women and pioneers live here, and so can any woman in the news whose story illustrates an issue.
