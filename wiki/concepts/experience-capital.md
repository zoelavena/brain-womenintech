# 💰 Experience capital

## ☕ The tea

Zoe's board: **"Women accumulate less experience capital."** Experience compounds like money. Every budget you own and every team you lead earns interest toward the next promotion. Women get handed the relationship work instead, so their balance grows more slowly, even when they work just as hard.

## 🧾 Receipts

See [[exposure-gap]]: budgets 27.8% vs 36.6%, and leading teams 59.3% vs 73.2%. *(Toast 2026)*
