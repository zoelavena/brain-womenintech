# 🕳️ Three kinds of gaps

## ☕ The tea

Not every gap is a skill gap. Usually it isn't, and that's the whole point.

| Gap | What it means | Lives on the tree at |
|---|---|---|
| **Evidence gap** | You do the work, but you can't show it | [[evidence-gap]] (4.4, 5.1) |
| **Exposure gap** | You haven't been given the opportunity | [[exposure-gap]] (3.4, 4.6, 5.4) |
| **Skill gap** | You don't know how yet | Not on the tree, which is the point |

## 🎨 From the original research

> "Skill gap that leadership thinks it's seeing is actually an exposure gap." ([[zoe-research-notes]])
