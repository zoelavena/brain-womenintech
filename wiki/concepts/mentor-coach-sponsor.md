# 🤝 Mentor vs coach vs sponsor

## ☕ The tea

These three get used interchangeably, and that mix-up is literally node 5.2 on the tree. Women get mentored. Men get sponsored.

| | What they do | Zoe's board |
|---|---|---|
| **Mentor** | Gives advice and shares experience | listed |
| **Coach** | Helps you find your own answers | listed |
| **Sponsor** | Spends their own capital to advocate for you where decisions get made | listed. In Triple Threat it's an **MG** skill. |

## 🧾 Receipts

- 43% of people have a senior leader actively advocating for them, and another 23% have a mentor who doesn't. *(Toast 2026, Recommendations and Method)*
