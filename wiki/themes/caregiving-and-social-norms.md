# 🍼 Caregiving and social norms: stepping away costs her more

## ☕ The tea

Women are 4x as likely as men to take a career break for full-time parenting, and the break costs them more when they come back.

## 🧾 Receipts

- **3.3:** 26.5% of women vs 6.0% of men took a career break for full-time parenting. *(WEF 2026, Section 2.1, Career pathways)*
- One year after a parenting break, 6.3% of women were promoted vs 9.1% of men. *(WEF 2026, Figure 2.7, senior individual contributors)*
- **4.1, unpaid care:** in the US, 15.1% of women's day vs 10.1% of men's. In Canada, 16.5% vs 11.6%. *(WEF 2026, Economy Profiles for the US and Canada)*
- Promotion gaps are smaller after a professional break than after a parenting break, so **the kind of break matters**. *(Zoe's board, summarizing WEF)*
