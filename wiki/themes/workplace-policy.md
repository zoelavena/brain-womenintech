# 📜 Workplace policy: men step away to grow, women step away to care

## ☕ The tea

The most common reason men take a career break is professional development. For women, it's full-time parenting. Policy decides which kind of break gets rewarded.

## 🧾 Receipts

- **4.2:** men's most common reason for a career break is professional development (19.8%). Women's is full-time parenting (26.5%). *(WEF 2026, Section 2.1, Career pathways)*
- **Feeling safe to raise gender issues:** 59% of women at employers that expanded DEI programs felt safe discussing gender-related issues, vs 29% where programs were reduced or eliminated. Association, not proof of cause. *(Web Summit Gender in Tech 2026, via Dataconomy, Oct 30, 2026)*
- **UK voluntary standards:** the UK government and industry (Women in Tech Taskforce) will co-create a voluntary Tech Forward Standard (workplace culture, incl. trusted harassment reporting routes) and Inclusive Investment Standard (deal sourcing, tracking women-led businesses from pitch to funding); formal launch in the coming months. *(GOV.UK, Oct 29, 2026)*
- **The numbers behind them:** women are 22% of the UK IT workforce (433,000 female IT specialists, 2025, BCS analysis of ONS Labour Force Survey); 2p of every £1 of UK VC goes to women-founded businesses (Investing in Women Code Annual Report 2026); 74% of call-for-evidence respondents said better workplace culture would support women's progression. TechFirst Women's Programme: 300 women into paid AI/cyber roles by March 2027. *(same source)*
