# Tanya Reilly

> 🪑 **Seat:** builder · **Known for:** naming "glue work" · **Shows up at:** [[evidence-gap]] (4.4 → 5.1)

## ☕ Her tea

Girl, she put a name on the thing so many women do and never get credit for. **Glue work** is the onboarding, the unblocking, the design reviews, and the keeping-everyone-aligned. It's technical leadership, and it often gets treated as "not engineering." She's basically the patron saint of our [[evidence-gap]].

## 👩🏽‍💻 Who she is
- Principal software engineer, and the writer behind the *No Idea* blog. She also wrote a book about staff engineering. *(noidea.dog/glue)*
- She gave "Being Glue" at the Lead Developer conference. An earlier version was accepted by Write/Speak/Code. *(same page; the date isn't stated)*

## 🍵 Her stories
- **The engineer who did everything right:** her talk follows an illustrative engineer who spends two years doing glue work and gets glowing feedback. When promotion comes around, she's told she isn't "an engineer," because she didn't write enough code. *(Being Glue)*
- She cites research showing women volunteer for non-promotable work more often than men, and get asked to do it more often too. *(Being Glue, citing an HBR study. Receipt not yet verified at the original source.)*

## 🔮 How I think she thinks
> The brain's perceived profile, read from her public work. 🟢 = she said it (cited). 🟡 = my read, inferred from the cited work.

- **She believes** glue work *is* leadership, and the problem is that orgs don't count it. 🟢 *(Being Glue)*
- **She frames problems** as systems design: if the promotion system rewards only code, people who do glue get filtered out. Fix the system, not the person. 🟡 *(inferred from her manager advice)*
- **Her instinct when something's unfair** is to make the invisible visible: track it, share it, credit it, and use titles that signal credibility. 🟢 *(Being Glue)*
- **What she'd tell a woman early in her career:** be strategic. Don't do *only* glue, keep building the skills that count, and choose roles on purpose. 🟢 *(Being Glue)*
