# Mary-Anne Williams

> 🪑 **Seat:** researcher · **Known for:** AI researcher — UNSW chair, founder of the UNSW Business AI Lab, Stanford and AAAI fellow; first Chief AI Scientist at Commonwealth Bank, leading its Distinguished AI Scientists · **Shows up at:** [[ai-and-the-future-of-women-in-tech]] (3.1)

## ☕ Her tea

Girl, she is the first seat in the AI-leadership wave with a team on the record — and she didn't get there by appointment alone. Williams is one of Australia's most recognised AI researchers, and she walked into the bank's first Chief AI Scientist role from a chair at UNSW, not from the CEO's office. One honest flag: the seat rests on her research record and the CBA role; her women-in-tech voice specifically is thin so far — she speaks on AI and its societal implications, not on women's advancement in tech. That's why she's brewing, not steeped.

## 👩🏽‍💻 Who she is
- Professor Mary-Anne Williams, appointed Commonwealth Bank of Australia's first Chief AI Scientist following an extensive global search; she started with CommBank on June 15, 2026. *(CommBank, May 19, 2026)*
- One of Australia's most internationally recognised AI researchers, with deep expertise spanning AI, agentic AI, human-AI collaboration, decision-making and innovation. *(CommBank, May 19, 2026)*
- Joined CommBank from UNSW, where she is the Michael J Crouch Chair for Innovation, Founder and Lead of the UNSW Business AI Lab, and Deputy Director of the UNSW AI Institute. *(CommBank, May 19, 2026)*
- A Fellow at Stanford University and a Fellow of the Association for the Advancement of Artificial Intelligence (AAAI). *(CommBank, May 19, 2026)*
- As Chief AI Scientist she leads CommBank's team of Distinguished AI Scientists — globally experienced researchers specialising in machine learning, responsible AI, AI security and generative AI — and plays a central role in shaping the bank's AI research agenda, attracting and developing AI talent, and supporting the continued application of responsible AI. *(CommBank, May 19, 2026)*
- Her career spans frontier research, global industry partnerships, robotics, startup advisory work, and the practical application of AI across business, government and society; her research focuses on transforming how organisations manage and orchestrate generative AI agents. *(CommBank, May 19, 2026)*

## 🍵 Her stories
- **The seat with a team attached:** unlike the three CAIO appointments the brain filed in November (Larkin at Eptura, Shaver at Davis Polk, Tanna at Barclays), Williams's role comes with a team on the record from day one — she leads CommBank's Distinguished AI Scientists. The bank's CAIO, Ranil Boteju, said she brings "a rare combination of academic leadership, commercial insight, and deep technical expertise." *(CommBank, May 19, 2026)*
- **The academic-to-industry move:** her appointment follows a model established by leading global technology and financial services companies in the US, recruiting senior AI leaders directly from academia — CommBank is the first Australian bank to do it that way. *(CommBank, May 19, 2026)*
