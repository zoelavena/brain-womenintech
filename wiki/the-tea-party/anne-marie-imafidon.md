## ☕ Her tea

Girl, she's the envoy — the UK government's Women in Tech Envoy, and the woman who built Stemettes so girls could see themselves in STEM long before a minister called. Her theory of change is the whole pipeline: entry, progression, leadership, entrepreneurship, all at once. One honest flag: her women-in-tech voice so far, in this brain, is one FE News interview and the government's announcement she responded to — that's the record this profile is built on, and it's why she's brewing, not steeped. Steeped waits for a fuller interview or talk in her own voice.

## 👩🏽‍💻 Who she is
- Dr Anne-Marie Imafidon MBE, co-founder and CEO of Stemettes, and the UK government's Women in Tech Envoy. *(FE News, Nov 2, 2026; GOV.UK, Oct 29, 2026)*
- Through Stemettes she has helped expose more than 70,000 young people across Europe to a more diverse and balanced STEM community. *(FE News, Nov 2, 2026)*
- As Envoy she is helping drive the government's women-in-tech agenda: two voluntary standards (the Tech Forward Standard on workplace culture and the Inclusive Investment Standard), a scale-up women's programme launching in 2027, and the TechFirst Women's Programme supporting 300 women into paid roles by March 2027. *(GOV.UK, Oct 29, 2026; FE News, Nov 2, 2026)*

## 🍵 Her stories
- **Naming the driver:** responding to the government's October 2026 announcement, she said harassment and discrimination remain key reasons for low participation — "Too many women have told us about facing misconduct like harassment and discrimination in tech. That is unacceptable and a key driver of poor participation in the sector." *(FE News, Nov 2, 2026; GOV.UK, Oct 29, 2026)*
- **The mandate:** she reads the government's call for evidence — 74% saying better workplace culture would support women's progression and leadership — as "a clear mandate" to work with industry and make tech somewhere women "stay, thrive and lead". *(FE News, Nov 2, 2026)*
- **The whole pipeline:** her frame is that meaningful change must create opportunities for women to enter the sector *and* lead — from early engagement with technology, through employment and progression, to entrepreneurship. *(FE News, Nov 2, 2026)*
