## The why behind it, in Zoe's words

> Observation: not many women are in leadership.
> Not because they don't have the skills, but because we don't see many examples.
> **The rung breaks.**
> How do we increase the representation?
> Recognize that as you go above the ladder, the more of an example you become for those women early in their career to aspire.
> **A small change we can do is to become sponsors for our kind.**

The career ladder on the board runs Software Engineer → Software Engineering Team Lead → Software Engineering Manager → Speaker.
