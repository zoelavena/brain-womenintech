# 🛰️ Radar: 2026-11-26

Scheduled nightly run. Window: everything published since the Nov 25 run. A quiet Thanksgiving night — one live update and two catch-ups the earlier runs missed. The live one arrived on Equal Pay Day itself: the Dutch pay-transparency law slipped again, its target now 1 July 2027 — and on the same day a Dutch tribunal ruled that grading a woman's pay from her salary history was indirect sex discrimination, so the courts are enforcing the principle the legislature hasn't passed yet. The catch-ups are the other side of the funding story: Serena Williams and Beth Ferreira launched a $200M-target fund to put women on the check-writing side of the 0.7%, and Harvard Shorenstein's own researchers named the gap behind the gap — women aren't in the room where AI's "bad things" get accepted.

| 1 | [The law slips again — on Equal Pay Day itself](https://acginter.com/en/pay-transparency-in-the-netherlands-2027-when-may-you-pay-one-employee-more-than-another/): on Nov 26, the Netherlands' Equal Pay Day, ACG International reports the minister is now aiming for the pay-transparency bill (36 949) to enter into force on **1 July 2027** — six months past the Jan 1 target filed yesterday, and more than a year past the EU's June 7, 2026 transposition deadline.
