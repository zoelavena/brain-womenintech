## 🌱 Didn't fit the tree

- **GHC 2026 canceled (finding 5).** No theme covers the gatherings and institutions themselves — the rooms where women get seen, hired, and sponsored. The tree assumes the rooms exist. This run says one of the biggest ones just closed for a year, and AnitaB.org says it is "evaluating strategic alternatives" for what comes next. **Decision for the curator:** does this deserve a new theme (gatherings / institutions / the rooms), or does it live on the [[sponsorship-gap]] and [[exposure-gap]] pages as context? Don't force it in either way.

## Caveats carried into the wiki

- Web Summit's survey reflects its own international community (1,000+ women; 37% aged 35-44; 33% founders/C-suite). It is not a representative sample of the whole tech workforce.
- The 2025 AI question asked about AI's *potential*; the 2026 question asked about its *observed impact*. The 75% → 31% contrast is a gap between promise and lived experience, not a measured decline in optimism.
- The 58% pay-fairness figure is perceived fairness, not payroll data. It does not by itself prove the actual gap widened.
