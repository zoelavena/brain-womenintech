## 🧠 The brain's read

The catch-up doesn't overturn anything — it deepens the two load-bearing claims. First, sponsorship is now a two-dataset story, not one: Toast's Canadian tech numbers and McKinsey/LeanIn's US/Canada cross-industry numbers agree that the gap is widest at entry level and that sponsors move promotions. Second, the ambition gap is support-contingent in two independent places: McKinsey/LeanIn (the gap falls away with equal sponsorship and manager support) and LeanIn's Gen Z survey (the gap closes when young women believe companies want them). That's a pattern, not a vibe — and it points the fix at the company's behavior, not women's drive.

The flexibility story also hardened: three datasets (McKinsey/LeanIn, Deloitte, Women in Digital) now say the same thing from different angles — flexibility is offered, and using it is quietly taxed. And the mid-career squeeze gained its mechanism: Deloitte's caring-break returns and Women in Digital's parenthood penalty name what happens in the years the Web Summit numbers only counted.
