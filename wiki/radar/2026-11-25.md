# 🛰️ Radar: 2026-11-25

Scheduled nightly run. Window: everything published since the Nov 24 run. The headline is the removal story with a receipt: one university cut a women's scholarship and 99 fewer women enrolled in a single year — while the office, the funding round, and the pay-transparency law all moved the same way. The return to the office brings back the office housework (on-site discrimination nearly doubles), the venture record is a record only if you count the megadeals (49% with at least one woman founder, 0.7% all-female), and the Dutch pay-transparency law — the compulsion test itself — will likely miss its own January 1 start. The platforms got their own receipt too: Equality Now maps how recommendation algorithms and ad money amplify misogyny, in 26 minutes flat.

## Curator decisions
1. **Seat Daniela Amodei?** She is the only woman among Anthropic's seven co-founders, holds roughly the same stake as each of the researchers who built the models, runs operations, hiring and culture at a 5,300-person company, and is now the world's 15th richest woman after the largest IPO in history. But the material in hand is IPO coverage, not her own words on women in tech. Seat on the record, or wait for her voice — the same bar as the CAIO seats, which weren't seated on an appointment alone?
2. **Track the RPI drop as a removal-story series?** One school, one scholarship, 99 fewer women in a year — a clean before-and-after for the programme cuts the ILO measured. Worth watching whether other schools that cut women's scholarships show the same drop, or is a single campus too thin to track?
3. **File the ACM elective-design study as a catch-up?** "Tailoring Elective Courses to Enhance Women's Participation in Computing" (Oct 15, 2026; 49,710 enrolments, 272 electives, nine institutions in Europe and North America) finds women enrol more in electives on human-computer interaction, ethics and applied software development — a curricular lever on the choice gap, and a candidate mechanism to sit beside belonging. It's a month old and outside tonight's window; file it, or leave it as an open question?
