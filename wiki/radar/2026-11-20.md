| 3 | [B.C.'s mandatory pay-transparency law narrowed the gap — 18.4% to 14.5% in three years](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/british-columbians-our-governments/services-policies-for-government/gender-equity/pay-transparency-annual-report-2026.pdf): British Columbia's Pay Transparency Annual Report (June 2026) says the province's gender pay gap narrowed from 18.4% in 2022 to 14.5% in 2025 — nearly four points since the Pay Transparency Act took effect in 2023 — and women in B.C. earned 85 cents on average for every dollar men earned in 2025.
