## 🌳 The spine
- [[five-whys-tree]]: Why are there so few women leading in tech? Root cause: no one is advocating for them in the room.

## 🔥 Themes on the root path
- [[sponsorship-gap]]: the root cause (4.5). Women are less likely to have a sponsor right where promotion decisions happen.
- [[evidence-gap]]: she does the work, it doesn't count as leadership (3.2 → 4.4 → 5.1).
- [[exposure-gap]]: women get relationships, men get resources (2.3 → 3.4 → 4.6 → 5.4).
- [[hiring-and-promotion-bias]]: same experience, picked less, and it's been getting worse since 2022 (3.5).

## 🌫️ Themes out of scope (kept on purpose)
- [[pipeline-and-education]]: fewer women start in tech, and they land in the lower-paid AI roles (2.1, 3.1).
- [[caregiving-and-social-norms]]: stepping away to care costs women more (3.3, 4.1).
- [[workplace-policy]]: men step away to grow, women step away to care (4.2).
- [[organizational-measurement]]: companies don't make the path visible (4.3).
