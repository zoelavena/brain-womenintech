## 🔥 Themes
- [[ai-and-the-future-of-women-in-tech]]: when AI roles open up, women land in the lower-paid ones.
- [[caregiving-and-social-norms]]: stepping away to care costs women more.
- [[evidence-gap]]: she does the work, and it doesn't count as leadership.
- [[exposure-gap]]: women get relationships, men get resources.
- [[hiring-and-promotion-bias]]: same experience, picked less, and it's been getting worse since 2022.
- [[organizational-measurement]]: companies don't make the path visible.
- [[pipeline-and-education]]: fewer women start in tech.
- [[sponsorship-gap]]: women are less likely to have a sponsor right where promotion decisions happen.
- [[workplace-policy]]: men step away to grow, women step away to care.
